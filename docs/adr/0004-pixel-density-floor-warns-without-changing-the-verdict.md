# A pixel-density floor warns without changing the verdict

The feet angle alone gave a confident Best to placements that cannot work in practice, such as a 2 MP camera at 9 m with about 70 px/m on the counting line and a "visible" face line at 8 px across a face. Each scene profile now sets a minimum pixel density for counting and for faces (starting at 80 and 200 px/m, to be tuned to the analytics vendor's spec). Below the counting floor the placement carries a low-density warning; below the face floor a visible face line is shown as too small. The verdict stays a judgement of the feet angle and framing.

## Considered Options

- **Counting line below the floor is Out of range**, like a person cut off in the frame: stricter, but it changes the verdict on a threshold we have not yet validated against the vendor's numbers.
