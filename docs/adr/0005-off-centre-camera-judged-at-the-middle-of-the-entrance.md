# An off-centre camera is judged at the middle of the entrance; the walkable ends warn

Real mounts rarely sit on the entrance centre line, and an off-centre camera sees one side of the doorway much closer than the other. The camera now has an offset from the entrance centre line and turns to aim at the middle of the entrance. The verdict is the feet angle there, at the floor distance from below the camera to the middle. The ends of the walkable width (the entrance less 0.35 m clear of each frame) are checked too: one outside the feet bands, or with a person there not wholly in frame, carries a warning and never changes the verdict.

## Considered Options

- **Judge across the walkable width, worst point decides**: built first, then dropped. The verdict, the side view and the feet distance window each described a different point, so the console showed a Best side view under an Out of range verdict and a distance window that did not match the drawing. Installers found it confusing.
- **Judge across the whole entrance**: stricter still; at 4 m a 5 m entrance would be Best only from 4.29 to 4.47 m, and the long-standing 4.60 m would fail at the frames, where nobody walks.
- **Middle of the entrance, ends warn** (chosen): one point decides, so every view agrees with the verdict, and the case the feature exists for (one side of the doorway far too shallow or cut off) is still called out.

## Consequences

- Solving for distance or height puts the middle of the entrance on the target angle. Off-centre the middle is farther than the counting line, so the solved line comes closer. Far enough off-centre no distance works, and the console says so.
- The warning follows ADR 0004: it informs, the verdict stays a judgement of the feet angle and framing in the middle.
- The entrance coverage check still warns without changing the verdict; it follows the panned view, which covers a shifted stretch of the counting line.
- The face line is judged along the middle of the entrance. Far enough off-centre there is no face-Best spot on it, and the console says so.
- The lookup table is unchanged: centred, with no entrance width.
